Import PNG

Publish

Run the full game through Steam. Select your work, press "Publish" and accept the terms. Share only art you own or have permission to publish.

Replace PNG

Select the existing work, use "Replace PNG", then "Publish". This updates its current Workshop page.

Subscriptions

Choose "Open in Steam", subscribe to a skin, return, press "Refresh" and "Use". Furniture is bought separately.

During visits, published skins are downloaded through Steam. Until a skin is available, the original appearance is shown. Local unpublished works are visible only to their author.
